## Final Grading Components [number of items × points per item]

- **Study Presentation** [2 × 10] — **20%**
- **Assignments** [3 × 10] — **30%**
- **Project** — **40%**
  - Project Proposal [1 × 5] — **5%**
  - Midway Report [1 × 10] — **10%**
  - Final Presentation [1 × 10] — **10%**
  - Final Paper [1 × 15] — **15%**
- **Participation** — **10%**
  - Completing ad-hoc activities announced in class — **2%**
  - Active participation in case study presentations — **4%**
  - Active participation in final presentations — **4%**

## Class Attendance Policy

This course takes place in person. While attendance is not formally tracked, 10% of the final grade is based on class participation. Participation may take various forms, including asking questions, contributing to discussions, and engaging in group activities.

## Late Policy

* **2-hour grace period:** All online assignments include an automatic two-hour grace period to accommodate technical issues. This window is applied automatically by the system, and no action is required from students.
* **Late penalty:** Assignments submitted after the grace period will incur a 10% deduction per day for up to five days (e.g., one day late results in a 10% reduction). Submissions more than five days late will receive a grade of zero.
* **Extenuating circumstances:** Whenever possible, documentation verifying extenuating circumstances may be requested to ensure equitable accommodation while respecting student privacy. If documentation cannot be provided, students are encouraged to contact the instructor as soon as possible. Extensions are generally not granted retroactively.
* **Final paper:** Extensions will not be granted for the final paper.

## Use of Generative AI

Students may use generative AI tools as collaborative aids, similar to human collaborators. However, directly requesting answers or copying solutions from generative AI tools is not permitted. Any use of generative AI must be clearly acknowledged. Failure to disclose AI use may be considered a violation of academic integrity policies.
