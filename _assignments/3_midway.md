# Midway presentation

During Week 10 (October 28), we will not have a regular lecture or tutorial. Instead, you will participate in a small-group presentation. Each student will briefly present their individual project to the group. For each student, approximately 15 minutes will be allocated:

- 10 minutes: Project presentation (with your laptop)
- 5 minutes: Questions, feedback, and discussion

The goal of this session is not to give a polished conference-style presentation, but to clearly explain your current project and receive useful feedback from your classmates. During the discussion, group members should actively ask questions and provide constructive feedback!

## What to cover in the presentation

- Research problem and motivation
  - What problem or topic does your project address?
  - Why is it worth investigating?

- Research question(s)
  - What specific question(s) are you trying to answer?

- Relevant background
  - Introduce the key previous research needed to understand your project.
  - Literature that informs your research questions and/or methodology.

- Data and methodology
  - Corpus you use
  - Your unit of analysis
  - Linguistic or computational measures
  - NLP or corpus-analysis methods

- Current progress
  - Explain what you have completed so far and what remains to be done.
  - Specific results are NOT required at this stage, but you are welcome to present preliminary results if you have them.

- Questions or challenges
  - You may highlight methodological questions, uncertainties, or challenges for which you would particularly like feedback.

## Group assignment and presentation schedule

I have posted the group assignments, including the designated **moderator**, under the *Midway Presentation* assignment tab in Lamakū. The moderator is responsible for facilitating the Q&A session following each presentation. Other group members are expected to actively participate by asking questions and providing feedback.

Each presenter will have 15 minutes for their presentation, followed by 10 minutes for Q&A, feedback, and completion of the Google Form. We will then move on to the next presenter.

### Participation during presentations

After listening to your classmates' presentations, you will be asked to complete and submit a short Google Form for each presentation.

The purpose of the form is to encourage active engagement with your classmates' research and provide constructive feedback that presenters can use to improve their projects.

Participation in the presentation sessions accounts for 4% of your course grade. Your submitted feedback forms will be used as evidence of participation. You will be assessed on the completion and thoughtfulness of your responses, rather than on whether your feedback is positive or negative.

## Submission

## Evaluation (5 points)

Your presentation will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

1. Research Focus and Progress (2 points) — Is the research problem clearly defined, and does the presentation show meaningful progress since the project proposal?
2. Development of Research Design (2 points) — Are the data, unit of analysis, measures, and planned methods sufficiently developed at this stage of the project?
3. Current Challenges and Next Steps (1 point) — Does the presentation identify remaining questions, challenges, or decisions and provide a clear plan for moving the project forward?
