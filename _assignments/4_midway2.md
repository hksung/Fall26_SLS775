# Midway report

The midway report is a *partial* draft of your final paper. For the midway report, please focus on the first three major sections:

1. Introduction
   This does not need to be fully polished or complete at this stage, but it should provide enough information for me to understand your research motivation, problem, and research question(s). Some of you may already have much of this material from your project proposal.

2. Background / Related Work
   This can be an expanded version of the annotated bibliography you submitted with your proposal.

3. Methodology
   This can be an expanded version of the Unit of Analysis and Measures section from your proposal. Please provide more detail about your data, unit(s) of analysis, measures, coding procedures, and/or computational methods, as relevant to your project.

4. (optional) You may also include a short section at the end listing questions, unresolved methodological issues, or areas where you would like feedback.

The following sections are expected for the *final* paper: Results, Discussion, Conclusion.

## Format options

You may choose either of the following formats.

### Option 1: Standard format

- **Font:** 12-point Times New Roman
- **Spacing:** Double-spaced
- **Length:** Up to 10 pages for the **final paper**, excluding references. Since the midway report covers only the first three sections, approximately **3–5 pages** would be a reasonable target, although this is flexible.
- **File type:** DOCX

### Option 2: ACL-style format

The ACL format is commonly used in computational linguistics and is provided as an alternative for those interested in practicing research writing in this field.

- **Length:** 4 pages for a short paper or 8 pages for a long paper (**final paper**, excluding references). A 4-page short paper is perfectly acceptable if you prefer a more concise format. For the midway report, aim for approximately 2–3 pages, depending on your progress and the scope of your project.
- **File type:** DOCX or PDF
- **Templates:**
  - [Download Word Template](../Files/acl.docx)
  - [ACL Style Files / Overleaf Template](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files) — an opportunity to practice LaTeX!

**Note:** The page lengths are guidelines, not strict requirements for the midway report. At this stage, I am more interested in seeing a clear research direction and an organized mehodology than a lengthy manuscript.
