# Final report

The final report is the completed version of your research paper, bringing together the work you have developed throughout the semester. This is the most important assignment in the course! :)
Your final report should include the following six sections, as typically found in academic research papers:

1. Introduction: Introduce your research, motivation, objectives, and research questions.
2. Background / Related Work: Review relevant literature and explain how your project builds on previous research.
3. Methodology: Describe your data, analytical procedures, and methods in sufficient detail.
4. Results: Present your findings, using tables and figures where appropriate.
5. Discussion: Interpret your findings in relation to your research questions and previous studies. Discuss any limitations.
6. Conclusion: Summarize your main findings, implications, and potential directions for future research.
7. (Optional: If you have a specific target audience, journal, or conference in mind for your paper, please let me know.)

## Format

Please continue using the format you selected for your midway report, unless you have a specific reason to change it.

### Option 1: Standard Format
- Font: 12-point Times New Roman
- Spacing: Double-spaced
- Length: Approximately 10 pages, excluding references. A longer paper is good if needed, but please keep it under 8,000 words - for the sake of my eyes.
- File type: DOCX

### Option 2: ACL-Style Format
- Length: 4 pages (short paper) or 8 pages (long paper), excluding references.
