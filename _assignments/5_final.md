# Final presentation

On the last day of class (12/9), each student will present their individual research project to the class. Each presentation will be allotted 10–12 minutes. Due to time constraints, auditing students are not required to present.

## Presentation format

Your presentation must consist of exactly 5 slides, following the structure below:

1. Research Questions: Introduce your research topic (briefly) and your research questions.
2. Data: Describe your dataset.
2. Methods: Describe analytical procedures.
4. Results: Present your key findings.
5. Implications: Discuss their implications and limitations.

Please adhere to the five-slide limit and focus on the most important aspects of your project.

## Participation During Presentations

While listening to your classmates' presentations, you will be asked to complete and submit a short Google Form for each presentation. As with the midway presentations, I will compile your feedback and share it with each presenter. (Participation in the final presentation sessions accounts for 2% of your course grade.)

## Submission

Please upload your presentation slides to Lamakū → Assignments → Final presentation by the day before class (12/8), so I can combine everyone's slides into a single presentation file (for smooth transitions between presentations).
